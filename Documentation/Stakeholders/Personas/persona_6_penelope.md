## Persona 6: Penelope

### Context and Role

- Role: Local Council Member
- Environment: In an office in the middle of the town
- Trigger: Needs local environmental data to support policies and help residents with any potential issues

### Goals and Success

#### Primary Goal
- Penelope requires a breif overview of the evironmental data at the current point in time
- A short paragraph describing how current readings relate to or differ from trends and how this will affect crops as a whole

#### Failures
- Cannot filter by location
- Data is not immediately displayed in application

### Pain Points
- Data needs to be analysed by her or her team 
- Data cannot be easily read and understood

### Workarounds
- Penelope hires a separate analyst and has to spend more time arranging meetings with them and is less cost effective

### Constraints and Non-Goals

#### Constraints
- Needs up-to-date, location specific information
- Needs a brief, digestible way of reading and understanding the information i.e. graphically or a brief paragraph describing current environemental factors and their effects

#### Non-Goals
- Does not need past data
- Does not need to access individual readings
- Will not need to identify plant species specifically
