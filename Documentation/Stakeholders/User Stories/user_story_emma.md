### User Stories: Emma

## User Story 1
**As a** farmer,
**I want to** scan a crop and get a plain language result,
**So that** i know whether it's affected and what to do next.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given i have a taken a photo of a crop, when the scan is complete, I can see a clear result that doesnt use technical, unclear, or hard to understand terminology. 
- Given that the crop is identifies as affected, when I view the result, I can see a simple recommended next step
    - eg: monitor, remove, isolate, etc.
