### User Stories: Penelope

## User Story 1
**As a** member of the council,
**I want to** see a summary of the current environmental climate,
**So that** I can advise residents on potential issues.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Given that the application has provided new informatiom, I can inform the residents of my council how it will impact their current situation and farming habits.
- Given that there has been a change in the environmental climate, I can see a summary of the situation and impacts to relay back to the other members of the council.
