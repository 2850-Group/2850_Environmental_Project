## Persona 5: Ben

### Context and Role

- Role: Head of a local farming community
- Environment: Coordinates multiple local farmers in his area
- Trigger: Multiple farmers in his community reporting crop disease simultaneously

### Goals and Success

#### Primary Goal
- To prevent local pests/disease from spreading throughout the local community by informing
non-affected members so they can prepare effectively

#### Failures
- Numerous farmers in the community lose their harvest because the warning came too late

### Pain Points
- No central place to see all member reports
- Hard to track individual phone calls and messages
- Cannot compare severity between farms

#### Workarounds
- Keeps manual records in a notebook
- Sends general warning messages to all members
- Relies on seasonal memory of past outbreaks

### Constraints and Non-Goals

#### Constraints
- Needs a simple overview dashboard, not individual plant-level detail
- Limited funding for expensive monitoring tools
- Members have different levels of technical ability

#### Non-Goals
- Does not need deep scientific breakdown of plant diseases
- Does not need national-level outbreak data - just local
