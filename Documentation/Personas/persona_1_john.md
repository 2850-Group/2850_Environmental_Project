## Persona 1: John Local Shop Owner 

### Context and Role

- Role: shop owner 
- Environment: Owns a village shop selling local produce
- Trigger: Understanding the incoming stock (when to check for potentail diseases and predicting the volume)

### Goals and Success

#### Primary Goal
- To be able to identify what crops he has purchased to sell that may have been affected by disease or pests
- To use current stats and crop infections to predict what stock he will have incoming

#### Failures
- Selling crops to the general public that are diseased which will lead to him having to close his shop
- Not planning for lower stock in poor hearvest seasons

### Pain Points
- No experience with agriculture, will not understand current statistics and their affect
- Cannot locate how to scan crops
- Cannot tell by sight whether a plant is infected

#### Workarounds
- Asks local farmers for analysis on what the data means
- Uses the internet to explain the statistics and symptoms of infected plants

### Constraints and Non-Goals

#### Constraints
- Needs a way to easily understand the current conditions and their affects
- Can scan plants and be given an answer of if they are diseased
- Can log the plants he's scanned so he can keep track and look back at the information provided

#### Non-Goals
- Does not need create alerts
- Does not need information for other locations
- Does not need historical data
