# Stakeholder Map

Key stakeholders for the Agricultural Monitoring & Detection System, how they are affected by the environmental challenge, and their relationship to the system.

The environmental challenge: pest and disease outbreaks cause major crop yield losses for small-scale farmers. Early detection is important to preventing spread across fields and supply chains. Existing tools are unaffordable or too technical for everyday users.

---

## Direct Users (use the app themselves)

| Stakeholder | How they are affected | What they need from the system |
|---|---|---|
| Smallholder Farmer | Crop losses due to undetected pest/disease outbreaks | Simple scan, plain language result, clear recommended action, works with low connectivity |
| Shop Owner | Risk of buying and selling diseased crops | Quick batch check, clear yes/no on whether crops are safe, flag batches by source |
| Distribution Manager | Risk of shipping contaminated crops across the supply chain | Map view of affected areas, ability to trace batches to source farm, proactive alerts |
| Agricultural Researcher | Needs data to analyse outbreak patterns and inform policy | Filtered historical data by region/crop/time, export functionality |



## Stakeholder Needs

### What matters most across all stakeholders
- Early detection before disease spreads
- Plain language , results and alerts must be understandable without agricultural expertise
- Accessibility , works for people with varying levels of technical confidence
- Mobile-first , primary users are in the field on smartphones
- Low connectivity support , rural areas often have poor or no signal

### What the system does not need to do for stakeholders
- Replace agricultural experts or extension officers
- Provide treatment or cure recommendations beyond basic next steps
- Monitor crops in other countries or outside the agreed project scope
